Privacy Policy

This page explains how information may be handled when you visit and use Tech Explained.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech Explained may handle information when visitors access and use this website.

We aim to respect the privacy of our visitors and to provide clear information about technologies that may be used on the website.

2. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you use the website, certain technical information may be processed automatically. This may include information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, pages visited, referring pages, and general information about how the website is used.

We do not require visitors to create an account simply to read the articles published on Tech Explained.

3. Cookies

Tech Explained may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, understand website usage, remember certain preferences, or support advertising services.

Cookies may be placed by the website itself or by third-party services used on the website.

4. Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors interact with the website. Analytics information can help us understand which pages are useful, identify technical issues, and improve the overall experience.

If an analytics service is enabled on the website, information may be processed according to the privacy policy of that service.

5. Advertising

Tech Explained may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google advertising services.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertising, subject to the choices and settings available to users in their respective regions.

For information about how Google handles information for advertising purposes, visitors can review Google's privacy information .

6. Third-Party Services

Some features of the website may rely on third-party services. These services may process certain information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Tech Explained does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

7. External Links

Our articles may occasionally contain links to external websites or services. Once you leave Tech Explained, the privacy practices of the external website apply.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website before providing personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Tech Explained is a general-interest technology website. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the website.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, technology, services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy-related matters concerning Tech Explained, you can contact us through our Contact page .
